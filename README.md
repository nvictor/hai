# Hai!

A simple and elegant attendance tracking application for macOS. Take rollcall with style!

![Hai Screenshot](docs/demo.png)

- Add attendees with a name and an avatar.
- Tap an attendee's card to mark them as called or absent.
- Reset all attendees with one click.
- Your list is saved on your Mac.

Requires macOS 26.0 or later and Apple Silicon.
